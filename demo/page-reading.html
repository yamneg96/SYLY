Keeping your laptop battery healthy

Test content written for the SYLY page-reading demo (FR-AD4). This page has no content file: the assistant reads this lesson from the page itself.

A laptop battery wears out slowly every time it is charged and emptied. A few simple habits can keep it useful for longer, which saves money and reduces electronic waste.

Keep the charge between 20 and 80 percent

Lithium-ion batteries age fastest when they sit at a full charge or are drained completely. For daily use, try to keep the battery between 20 and 80 percent. Many laptops have a setting that stops charging at 80 percent; turn it on if you usually work plugged in.

Avoid heat while charging

Heat is the biggest enemy of a battery. Do not charge the laptop on a bed, a pillow or in direct sunlight, because the air vents get blocked and the battery gets hot. Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface so that air can flow underneath it.

Storing a laptop you do not use

If you will not use the laptop for several weeks, charge the battery to about half, switch the laptop off completely and keep it in a cool, dry place. Check it every few months and charge it back to half if it has dropped.

When to replace the battery

Replace the battery when the laptop lasts much less than half of its original time on a full charge, when the battery case swells, or when the laptop shuts down suddenly with charge left. A swollen battery is a safety risk: stop using it and take it to a repair shop.
